# pitch

Pitch is frequency as the ear ranks it: from low to high. Ressac writes it as a MIDI note (`midinote`, 60 = middle C), as semitones from a root (`n`, `note`), as a scale degree (`degree`, `scale`) or as raw hertz (`freq`).

See also: frequency, semitone, scale, fundamental
