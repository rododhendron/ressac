# envelope

Without an envelope a synth would just switch on and off. `env_perc` gives a percussive hit, `env_adsr` a sustained note, `env_linen` a hold with fades. Envelopes also drive filters (a cutoff that drops over the note is the acid sound).

See also: ADSR, attack, decay, sustain, release
