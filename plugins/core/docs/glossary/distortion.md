# distortion

Every distortion is a shape applied to the signal. `tanh_drive` is soft and musical, `clip2` hard and buzzy, `fold2` wild and inharmonic, `crush` and `coarse` are digital and lo-fi. `shape` is SuperDirt's waveshaper.

See also: saturation, clipping, wave folding, bit crushing
